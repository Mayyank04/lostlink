# LostLink 2.0 — Final Comprehensive System Audit & Verification Matrix

## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Methodology
This document presents the final engineering audit of **LostLink 2.0 (Smart Multi-College Lost & Found Platform)**. In accordance with the Phase 6 Final Master Prompt, every subsystem was independently tested against the live codebase, the PostgreSQL database, and the active Kubernetes cluster (`lostlink-cluster`).

No feature is marked as passed without direct empirical execution evidence.
